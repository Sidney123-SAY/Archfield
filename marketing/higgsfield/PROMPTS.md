# Higgsfield : images et vidéos pour l'accueil « la maison se construit »

À faire dans l'application Higgsfield, toujours dans le même ordre. Quand c'est fini, dites « c'est fait » à Claude :
il récupère les résultats avec le connecteur Higgsfield et les met sur le site.

Réglages communs : format **16:9**, la meilleure résolution disponible, **sans son**.
Gardez exactement la même maison et le même cadrage : chaque image utilise la précédente comme **référence**.

## Étape A : les images (modèle Nano Banana Pro, 2K)

**A1. La maison finie** (sans référence)

> Photorealistic architectural photograph of a newly finished modern two-storey family villa in West Africa. Rendered concrete walls painted warm off-white, terracotta hip roof, large dark aluminium windows, a covered entrance porch with two white columns and a small balcony above it, low boundary wall, palm trees and bougainvillea, red laterite ground with fresh paving. Straight-on front view at eye level, house centred, wide angle, golden late-afternoon light, clear sky. No people, no text, no watermark.

**A2. Le plan lumineux** (référence : A1)

> Same house, exact same camera position and framing as the reference, shown as an architect's glowing holographic blueprint: thin luminous electric-blue wireframe lines tracing every wall, window, column and the roof, floating over a dark navy night scene with faint silhouettes of the palm trees. No people, no text, no watermark.

**A3. Les fondations** (référence : A1)

> Same plot, exact same camera position and framing as the reference, at the start of construction: the house is not built yet. Cleared red laterite ground, freshly poured grey concrete foundations tracing the footprint of the house, steel rebar starter bars sticking up where the columns will be, stacks of grey concrete blocks and cement bags, the same palm trees, golden late-afternoon light. Photorealistic construction-site photo. No people, no text, no watermark.

**A4. Les murs** (référence : A1)

> Same house, exact same camera position and framing as the reference, during construction: ground-floor walls of raw grey hollow concrete blocks, reinforced concrete columns with rebar sticking out of the top, empty window and door openings, simple scaffolding, cement bags and sand piles on red laterite ground, the same palm trees, golden late-afternoon light. Photorealistic construction-site photo. No people, no text, no watermark.

**A5. Le gros œuvre** (référence : A1)

> Same house, exact same camera position and framing as the reference, structural shell complete but unfinished: two storeys of raw grey concrete blocks and concrete columns, upper floor slab poured, roof frame being installed, no render, no paint, no windows fitted yet, scaffolding on one side, the same palm trees, golden late-afternoon light. Photorealistic construction-site photo. No people, no text, no watermark.

**A6. Le salon fini** (sans référence)

> Photorealistic interior photograph of the finished living room of a modern villa in West Africa: large light porcelain floor tiles, warm white walls, a tall dark aluminium sliding window onto a garden with palm trees, ceiling fan, beige sofa with wax-print cushions, wooden coffee table, indoor plants, soft natural daylight. Straight-on view of the window wall, eye level, wide angle. No people, no text, no watermark.

**A7. Le salon en gros œuvre** (référence : A6)

> Same room, exact same camera position and framing as the reference, during construction: bare grey concrete block walls, rough concrete floor, window opening without frame, electrical conduits on the walls, sunlight streaming in, a few cement bags. Photorealistic. No people, no text, no watermark.

**A8. Le salon enduit** (référence : A6)

> Same room, exact same camera position and framing as the reference, walls plastered and primed white, window frame installed, floor tiles half laid, no furniture yet. Photorealistic. No people, no text, no watermark.

## Étape B : les vidéos de passage (modèle Seedance 2.0, 5 s, 1080p, son coupé)

Chaque vidéo part d'une image (**start image**) et arrive à la suivante (**end image**). Caméra fixe : la maison ne bouge pas, seul le chantier avance.
